⚠️ The "Headline Price Trap" Explained

Consider a grower in Sehore with 65 quintals of Sharbati Wheat Grade A ready for sale:

Azadpur Mandi (780 km away) Deceptive Headline
Headline Quote: ₹3,290 / Quintal
₹2,728.10 / qtl real net
  • Gross Lot Revenue (65 qtl @ ₹3,290) ₹2,13,850
  • Freight Transport (780 km @ ₹0.85/km/qtl) -₹43,095
  • APMC Commission (3%) + Cess (1.5%) -₹9,623
  • Hamali Labor + Gunny Bags -₹2,990
  • Total Deductions Paid -₹55,708 (26%)
  • Final Realized Cash-in-Hand ₹1,58,142
Indore APMC (85 km away) Smart Net Choice
Headline Quote: ₹2,980 / Quintal (-₹310 lower!)
₹2,770.35 / qtl real net
  • Gross Lot Revenue (65 qtl @ ₹2,980) ₹1,93,700
  • Freight Transport (85 km @ ₹0.85/km/qtl) -₹4,696
  • APMC Commission (2%) + Cess (1%) -₹5,811
  • Hamali Labor + Bags -₹2,470
  • Total Deductions Paid -₹13,627 (7%)
  • Final Realized Cash-in-Hand ₹1,80,073
💡 Result: Selling locally at Indore yielded +₹21,931 MORE net cash in hand than sending the truck to Azadpur, despite Azadpur boasting a headline quote ₹310 higher!
